At the heart of the village

Your everyday stop for something lovely.

From a quick coffee and a bacon roll to a box of bakes for the table, there’s a little something for the middle of an ordinary day and the days worth celebrating.

The bakery opened in September 2022 and has become part of village life. It also received a community service award from Breckland Council.

✳A warm welcome, whatever the occasion.

A little something for everyone

What’s good today?

Fresh-from-the-oven favourites, an easy lunch, or a sweet treat for later.

01 / baked✳

For the sweet tooth

Brownies, blondies, Danish pastries and fresh cream cakes. The kind of choice that makes the walk home feel very long.

02 / lunchtime♡

For a proper lunch

Freshly made rolls and baguettes, pasties, jacket potatoes and salads. There’s a bacon roll for slower mornings, too.

03 / little extras☕

For the journey

Hot drinks, ice slushies and a loaf of fresh bread delivered daily from Watton Bakery.
